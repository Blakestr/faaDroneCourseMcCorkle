> [!faq] Types of Airport
>
> _Civil -_ open to the public
> _Military (Federal)_ - no open to the public
> _Private_ - requires permission from the owner

> [!tip] Towered Airport
>
> >
>
> - Has an operating control tower
> - Referred to as ATC (**AIR TRAFFIC CONTROL**)
> - ATC responsible for **safe, orderly, and expeditious FLOW** **of air traffic**
> - Communication with ATC is required for **ALL** _manned_ aircraft

> [!info] Non-towered Airport
>
> - **NO** operating control tower
> - found at **smaller** airports with low traffic
> - no one guiding pilots in
> - radio communication is **NOT** required at those airports (this makes having eyes and spotters more necessary)
> - Radio calls are recommended on the following
>   - **Common Traffic Advisory Frequency** (CTAF) (more likely use for portable radios)
>   - **Universal Integrate Community** (UNICOM)

> [!tldr] True VS Magnetic North
>
> Oriented ourselves by two main references :
>
> - _True Norht_ the physical Earth's North Pale (Santa) Most maps are oriented in relation to True North
> - _Magnetic North_ - the location of Earth's magnetic  field's northing position
>   - NOT THE SAME AS THE NORTH POLE
>   - Magnetic north changes over time
>   - Magnetic compasses align with this, so do aircraft instrument
>   - **COMPASS = Magnetic| Map = True North**
>     THESE ARE NO LOCATED AS THE SAME POLE
>     ![[Attachments/Pasted image 20261118134057.png]]
>
> _Variation_ - difference between true and magnetic north (in degrees and minutes)
>
> Variation CHANGES dependong on your **LOCATION** on earth and is identified as **East** or **West**
>
> To convert from True to Magnetic, ADD/SUBTRACT variation
>
> "East is LEAST, West is BEST" (subtract east, add west)"
>
> _Isogonic Lines_ - Lines of variation (shown on aeronautical charts)
